What does opponent rating mean?

Opponent rating is the average current model strength of the teams already faced this season, weighted toward more recent games. Positive means tougher-than-average opposition; negative means weaker-than-average opposition. It describes schedule difficulty. Your team's own rating already includes the opponent adjustment.

What is behind the ranking?

Current-season final scores, game dates, stable team IDs and home/neutral venue status. Every team is fitted together, so a result against a strong opponent means something different from the same result against a weak one. Older games gradually lose weight; ratings with little evidence shrink toward the league average.

margin = clip(home score − away score, −cap, +cap)
weight = 2^(−age in days / half-life)
fit all ratings and the home adjustment to minimize:
Σ weight × [margin − (home rating − away rating + home adjustment × venue)]²
+ λ × Σ rating² + λ_home × home adjustment²

Venue is 0 for neutral games and 1 otherwise. The model uses no bookmaker lines, injuries, player projections, possession counts, EPA or expected goals. Those are candidates for later testing, rather than hidden inputs.
